Generator catalogue

Every generator the Inkscape MCP can draw, three seeds or variants each. To use one, press copy under an example and paste it into Claude with where it should go — e.g. "chain seed 2 as a second protein at the bottom right". Add words like denser, paler or thinner to adjust it, or "clip it into the cell" / "use it as a pattern fill on the box".

Colours and line weights come from your style tokens (figures/style.json); regenerate this page after changing them: python -m inkscape_mcp.generators.catalogue docs/catalogue.html
